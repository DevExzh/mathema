Embedded Latin Modern fonts
===========================

Mathema's optional `render` feature embeds 13 OpenType font files. These
are third-party works from the Latin Modern and Latin Modern Math projects.
Their exact upstream license texts are included as
`LICENSE-LATIN-MODERN.txt` and `LICENSE-LATIN-MODERN-MATH.txt`.
The GUST Font License permits redistribution under the terms of the
LaTeX Project Public License. The complete LPPL 1.3c text is included as
`LICENSE-LPPL-1.3c.txt`.

The upstream project manifests are included verbatim as
`MANIFEST-Latin-Modern.txt` and `MANIFEST-Latin-Modern-Math.txt`.
They contain the project copyright, authors, maintenance status, current
maintainers, and the font and file names covered by each distribution.

--------------------------------

  Designer (Computer Modern Family of Fonts): Donald E. Knuth
  Authors: Bogus\l{}aw Jackowski and Janusz M. Nowacki
  Version: 2.004
  Date: 30 X 2009

  Copyright 2003--2009 by B. Jackowski and J.M. Nowacki
  (on behalf of TeX USERS GROUPS).
  This work is released under the GUST Font License.
  This work has the LPPL maintenance status "maintained".
  The Current Maintainer of this work is Bogus\l{}aw Jackowski
  and Janusz M. Nowacki.

The 12 included text files are the exact byte-for-byte files installed by
TeX Live under `/usr/share/texmf/fonts/opentype/public/lm`:

  src/render/fonts/lmmono10-italic.otf
  src/render/fonts/lmmono10-regular.otf
  src/render/fonts/lmmonolt10-bold.otf
  src/render/fonts/lmmonolt10-boldoblique.otf
  src/render/fonts/lmroman10-bold.otf
  src/render/fonts/lmroman10-bolditalic.otf
  src/render/fonts/lmroman10-italic.otf
  src/render/fonts/lmroman10-regular.otf
  src/render/fonts/lmsans10-bold.otf
  src/render/fonts/lmsans10-boldoblique.otf
  src/render/fonts/lmsans10-oblique.otf
  src/render/fonts/lmsans10-regular.otf

The preferred family names recorded in the files are Latin Modern Mono,
Latin Modern Mono Light, Latin Modern Roman, and Latin Modern Sans. The
two `lmmonolt10` files are specifically Latin Modern Mono Light.
